SPACE CADASTRE

The term “space” is typically defined as a three dimensional vacuum that encompasses all matter and energy within the physical structure of the universe.
This concept is combined with time to form the fabric of what is known as “spacetime”, a four-dimensional continuum
Orbital Space: This term is used to describe the region in which space objects move in specific orbits around the Earth.
This region is of critical importance for spacecraft and satellites. An orbit is defined as a closed path that a space object follows under the influence of Earth’s gravity. Low Earth orbit (LEO) is a region of space with an altitude of 200 to 2,000 kilometers, utilized for low-altitude spacecraft such as communication and meteorological satellites, as well as the ISS.
